PCB Bancorp Reports Record Earnings for Q3 2026
Los Angeles, California, - October 29, 2026 - PCB Bancorp (the “Company”) (NASDAQ: PCB), the holding company of PCB Bank (the “Bank”), today reported net income available to common shareholders of $12.1 million, or $0.85 per diluted common share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $10.4 million, or $0.73 per diluted common share, for the previous quarter and $11.3 million, or $0.78 per diluted common share, for the year-ago quarter.
Q3 2026 Highlights
•Net income available to common shareholders totaled $12.1 million, or $0.85 per diluted common share, for the current quarter;
•Provision (reversal) for credit losses was $(705) thousand for the current quarter compared with $926 thousand for the previous quarter and $(381) thousand for the year-ago quarter;
•Allowance for Credit Losses (“ACL”) on loans to loans held-for-investment ratio was 1.20% at September 30, 2026 compared with 1.18% at June 30, 2026, and 1.20% at September 30, 2025;
•Net interest income was $28.8 million for the current quarter compared with $27.5 million for the previous quarter and $27.0 million for the year-ago quarter. Net interest margin was 3.35% for the current quarter compared with 3.33% for the previous quarter and 3.28% for the year-ago quarter;
•Gain on sale of loans was $1.6 million for the current quarter compared with $1.2 million for the previous quarter and $1.6 million for the year-ago quarter;
•Total assets were $3.54 billion at September 30, 2026, an increase of $67.5 million, or 1.9%, from $3.47 billion at June 30, 2026, an increase of $255.9 million, or 7.8%, from $3.28 billion at December 31, 2025, and an increase of $174.2 million, or 5.2%, from $3.36 billion at September 30, 2025;
•Loans held-for-investment were $2.84 billion at September 30, 2026, a decrease of $87.2 million, or 3.0%, from $2.93 billion at June 30, 2026, but an increase of $24.5 million, or 0.9%, from $2.82 billion at December 31, 2025 and an increase of $92.3 million, or 3.4%, from $2.75 billion at September 30, 2025; and
•Total deposits were $3.08 billion at September 30, 2026, an increase of $153.9 million, or 5.3%, from $2.92 billion at June 30, 2026, an increase of $281.2 million, or 10.1%, from $2.80 billion at December 31, 2025, and an increase of $163.1 million, or 5.6%, from $2.91 billion at September 30, 2025.
Henry Kim, President and CEO, commented, “We delivered another quarter of strong financial performance despite a competitive operating environment and continued interest rate volatility. Our results reflect disciplined execution across the organization, highlighted by record earnings, solid core deposit growth, and an improvement in net interest margin.
During the quarter, we remained focused on prudent balance sheet management and long-term risk-adjusted returns. As part of that strategy, we selectively reduced certain large commercial lending relationships that no longer met our risk and return objectives. While these actions contributed to lower loan balances, they strengthened our liquidity position and reinforced the overall quality of our balance sheet.
Our strong retail deposit growth, solid capital position, and disciplined credit culture continue to provide a foundation for sustainable growth. As we look ahead to the remainder of 2026 and into 2027, we believe we are well positioned to deploy our excess liquidity, capitalize on attractive growth opportunities, and continue delivering long-term value for our shareholders.”
