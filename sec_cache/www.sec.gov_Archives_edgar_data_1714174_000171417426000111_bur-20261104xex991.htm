Burford Reports 3Q26 and YTD26 Financial Results

Burford Capital Limited (“Burford”), the leading global finance and asset management firm focused on law, announces its unaudited financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 (“3Q26” and “YTD26”, respectively). The full detailed presentation of Burford’s 3Q26 and YTD26 financial results can be viewed at http://investors.burfordcapital.com.

Christopher Bogart, Burford Capital’s Chief Executive Officer, commented:

“Burford had its strongest quarter this year, with robust portfolio activity and good cash generation. Total revenues of $135 million would have been well over $200 million1 if not for the non-cash increase in market discount rates. We also achieved $100 million of de-leveraging and retired our nearest-term debt maturity. We closed the quarter with more than a half-billion dollars of cash on hand.

“After the quarter ended, we also brought in a further $64 million in cash from the resolution of a 2015 vintage matter, generating a 12x return2.”

About Burford Capital

Burford Capital is the leading global finance and asset management firm focused on law. Its businesses include litigation finance and risk management, asset recovery and a wide range of legal finance and advisory activities. Burford is publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE: BUR) and the London Stock Exchange (LSE: BUR) and works with companies and law firms around the world from its global network of offices.

For more information, please visit www.burfordcapital.com.
