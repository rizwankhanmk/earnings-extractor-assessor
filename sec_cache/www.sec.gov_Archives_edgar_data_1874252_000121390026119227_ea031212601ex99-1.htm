NORWALK, Connecticut, November 12, 2026 -- Quantum Cyber N.V. (Nasdaq: QUCY) (“Quantum Cyber” or the “Company”), a Nasdaq-listed autonomous defense technology company assembling an AI-powered System-of-Systems platform for drone warfare, counter-UAS, and border security applications, today announced that it has filed its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. The filing reports the Company’s first revenue since its strategic pivot to autonomous defense and discloses an $8.0 million funding commitment from the Company’s Chief Executive Officer and Chair of the Board, David Lazar.

 

“The third quarter of 2026 is the quarter in which Quantum Cyber crossed from a pre-revenue strategy into an operating company,” said David Lazar. “We generated our first revenue, we installed the first production equipment at our Bridgeport facility, and we resolved a legacy legal claim that had overhung our capital structure. In the Company’s view, each of these is a building block toward the vertically integrated autonomous defense manufacturer we have described to the market.”

 

“The $8.0 million funding commitment I provided to the Company on November 11, 2026 is intended to reinforce the balance sheet while we complete the build-out of the Bridgeport facility and continue to advance the Project LightShift quantum antenna program under our newly expanded field of use. We believe Quantum Cyber is entering 2027 as an operating, revenue-generating business with the manufacturing footprint, the technology licenses, and the capital on hand to execute the next phase of its plan.”

 

~$1.0 Million of First Revenue From Continuing Operations

 

For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported $966,833 of revenue, compared with no revenue from continuing operations in the first and second quarters of 2026 and in the prior-year periods. The revenue was generated by the precision metal stamping and fabrication business operating out of the Company’s Bridgeport, Connecticut facility, which the Company acquired on July 15, 2026 from Arcade Technology LLC and Arcade Realty LLC. Revenue was spread across three product lines, electrical components, tooling, and general metal stamping products, with four customers each accounting for between 10% and 15% of quarterly revenue.

 

 

 

 

In the Company’s view, the transition from a pre-revenue platform-development story to an operating business with inventory, accounts receivable, manufacturing assets, and a diversified customer base in a single quarter represents a meaningful inflection point in the execution of its vertical integration strategy.

 

$8.0 Million Funding Commitment From Chief Executive Officer

 

On November 11, 2026, Mr. Lazar provided the Company with a funding commitment of up to $8.0 million through November 30, 2027. The Company believes the commitment is intended to supplement its working capital as it continues to build out its autonomous defense manufacturing capacity at the Bridgeport facility and to advance its Quantum Drones programs. A copy of the Funding Commitment Letter is filed as Exhibit 10.4 to the Form 10-Q.

 

Bridgeport Facility Operational

 

The Company’s Bridgeport facility continues to operate as the revenue-generating Arcade Metal Stamping business while the Company builds out the site for drone manufacturing. In August 2026, the Company completed the installation of an 80-unit 3D printing production farm at the facility. The Company currently expects to begin manufacturing and selling drones in early 2027, subject to further build-out of production lines, procurement of equipment, and completion of commercial prototypes.

 

At September 30, 2026, the Company’s balance sheet reflected approximately $2.78 million of property, plant and equipment, $929,606 of inventory, $643,386 of accounts receivable, and $534,000 of goodwill, substantially all associated with the Bridgeport operations. These line items did not appear on the Company’s December 31, 2025 or March 31, 2026 balance sheets.

 

Expansion of the Quantum Antenna License Field of Use

 

On November 6, 2026, the Company’s wholly owned subsidiary, Quantum Drones Corporation, and Project LightShift, Inc. entered into a First Amendment to their June 2026 exclusive worldwide license agreement covering Project LightShift’s patent-protected quantum photonic array, or quantum antenna, technology. The First Amendment expands the licensed field of use beyond unmanned aerial vehicles to also include unmanned surface and underwater vehicles, maritime platforms, homeland security, and law enforcement applications. The Company believes this expansion materially broadens the addressable market for the licensed quantum antenna technology across air, land, and sea autonomous platforms.

 

Resolution of Legacy Boustead Claims

 

On October 15, 2026, the Company entered into a Settlement Agreement and General Release with Boustead Securities, LLC resolving the action filed by the Company in the Supreme Court of the State of New York, New York County, and the related FINRA arbitration previously initiated and withdrawn by Boustead. Under the agreement, Quantum Drones Corporation paid Boustead $250,000, and the parties agreed to mutual general releases. The Boustead Settlement Agreement also terminates Boustead’s April 2021 engagement letter with the Company, including any related rights to fees, compensation, right of first refusal, or tail compensation in connection with any future financing or transaction by the Company. In the Company’s view, this removes a legacy overhang from its capital structure and future financing activities.

 

Balance Sheet

 

At September 30, 2026, the Company reported total assets of approximately $18.8 million, total shareholders’ equity of approximately $16.2 million, and cash and marketable securities of approximately $5.0 million. Shareholders’ equity at December 31, 2025 was approximately $0.6 million, representing an increase of approximately 2,600% over the period. During the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company received approximately $22.2 million of net proceeds from financing activities, including approximately $15.2 million from the exercise of warrants and $6.0 million from the issuance of preferred shares.

About Quantum Cyber N.V.

 

Quantum Cyber N.V. (Nasdaq: QUCY) is assembling an AI-powered, quantum-accelerated System-of-Systems autonomous defense platform that integrates drone warfare, counter-UAS, autonomous naval mine countermeasures, EMP shielding, anti-drone ammunition, command-and-control, and quantum antenna applications under a single Nasdaq-listed company. The Company acquires, licenses, and develops combat-proven autonomous technologies, deploying them as a coordinated, multi-domain portfolio across air, land, and sea. For more information, visit www.quantum-cyber.ai.
