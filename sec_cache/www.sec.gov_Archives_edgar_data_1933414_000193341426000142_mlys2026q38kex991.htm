Mineralys Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Update
– Commercialization plans for lorundrostat are on track ahead of PDUFA target date of December 22, 2026 –
– New analyses from the pivotal Launch-HTN trial showed 46% of participants with uncontrolled or resistant hypertension receiving lorundrostat 50 mg achieved the AHA/ACC guideline systolic blood pressure target of <130 mmHg at Week 12 –
– Transform-HTN open-label extension trial data selected for late-breaking presentation at American Heart Association Scientific Sessions 2026 –
– Conference call today at 4:30 p.m. ET –
RADNOR, Pa. — November 4, 2026 — Mineralys Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: MLYS), a biopharmaceutical company focused on developing medicines to target hypertension and aldosterone-related adverse outcomes in comorbid conditions such as chronic kidney disease (CKD), obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) and other diseases driven by dysregulated aldosterone, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.
“With approximately 20 million adults in the U.S. living with uncontrolled or resistant hypertension, despite treatment, there is a significant need for new treatment options. As we approach the December 22nd PDUFA target date for lorundrostat, our focus is on completing our launch preparations so that we are ready to reach patients quickly, pending approval,” said Jon Congleton, Chief Executive Officer of Mineralys. “We also continue to highlight the data from our clinical trials in meetings with physicians and through presentations at scientific congresses, including our recent updates at AHA-HTN and ASN Kidney Week, and our upcoming presentation at the AHA Scientific Sessions. We continue to receive positive feedback from providers, payers and people living with hypertension, and we look forward to the potential to bring lorundrostat to millions of patients in need.”
Recent Highlights and Upcoming Milestones
•Lorundrostat New Drug Application (NDA) — The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) continues its review of the NDA for lorundrostat for the treatment of hypertension in combination with other antihypertensive drugs, with a Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA) target date of December 22, 2026.


•New Launch-HTN and Explore-OSA Data at the American Heart Association Hypertension Scientific Sessions (AHA-HTN) and American Society of Nephrology (ASN) Kidney Week 2026
◦A new analysis of the pivotal Phase 3 Launch-HTN trial showed that at Week 12, 46% of lorundrostat-treated participants (50 mg) achieved the AHA/ACC guideline-recommended office systolic blood pressure (SBP) target of <130 mmHg, with more than two-thirds achieving an SBP reduction of ≥10 mmHg. Participants had uncontrolled or treatment-resistant hypertension despite treatment with two to five antihypertensive therapies.
◦An additional new analysis from Launch-HTN demonstrated that lorundrostat achieved similar blood pressure reductions in participants with and without CKD, with consistent reductions across Stages 1-3 CKD. Lorundrostat also reduced albuminuria by 52% versus placebo, validating similar findings in a prior trial in participants with established CKD.
◦Final results from the Phase 2 Explore-OSA trial in overweight or obese adults with moderate-to-severe OSA and hypertension showed that lorundrostat 50 mg taken once daily in the evening reduced morning SBP by 11.1 mmHg at Week 4 (p<0.0001), compared with a 1.0 mmHg reduction with placebo (p=NS). No measurable change in apnea-hypopnea index was observed. Lorundrostat exhibited a favorable safety and tolerability profile, with no evidence of serious safety events, including hyperkalemia.
•Transform-HTN Open-Label Extension Trial — New long-term efficacy and safety data from the ongoing Transform-HTN open-label extension trial will be featured in a late-breaking presentation at the American Heart Association Scientific Sessions 2026, taking place November 6-9 in Chicago, IL.
•Commercial Launch Readiness — The Company is advancing its commercial launch preparations and now has an expanded medical affairs team and sales force in place. The Company continues to engage with leading hypertension experts and payers. Brand marketing preparations are well underway, and the Company is finalizing a full-service patient support program designed to ease access for physicians, their office staff and patients.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
Cash, cash equivalents and investments were $624.7 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $656.6 million as of December 31, 2025. The Company believes that its current cash, cash equivalents and investments will be sufficient to fund planned operations, including the commercial launch of lorundrostat, into 2028.
Research and development (R&D) expenses for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, were $22.7 million, compared to $31.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The decrease in R&D expenses was primarily due to $8.5 million of lower preclinical and clinical costs resulting from


the conclusion of the lorundrostat pivotal program in the second quarter of 2025 and $0.3 million of decreased clinical supply, manufacturing, regulatory and other costs.
General and administrative (G&A) expenses were $37.4 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $9.7 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase in G&A expenses was primarily due to investments to expand the Company’s operations in preparation for commercialization, including $15.4 million in higher professional fees and $11.4 million in increased personnel-related expenses resulting from headcount growth and increased compensation, as well as $0.9 million of increased other administrative expenses.
Total other income, net was $2.8 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $4.2 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was primarily due to $3.2 million of increased interest expense related to the senior secured term loan entered into in June 2026, partially offset by $1.8 million of increased interest earned on investments as a result of higher average cash balances during the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Net loss was $57.3 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $36.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to the factors impacting the Company’s expenses described above.

About Lorundrostat
Lorundrostat is an investigational, proprietary, orally administered, highly selective aldosterone synthase inhibitor being developed for the treatment of uncontrolled hypertension (uHTN) or resistant hypertension (rHTN), as well as related comorbidities, such as chronic kidney disease, obstructive sleep apnea and other diseases driven by dysregulated aldosterone. Lorundrostat was designed to reduce aldosterone levels by inhibiting CYP11B2, the enzyme responsible for its production. Lorundrostat has 374-fold selectivity for aldosterone-synthase inhibition versus cortisol-synthase inhibition in vitro, has an observed half-life of 10-12 hours and demonstrated a 40-70% reduction in plasma aldosterone concentration in participants with hypertension.
Mineralys has completed six late-stage clinical trials of lorundrostat supporting its efficacy and safety profile while also validating aldosterone as an integral therapeutic target in uHTN and rHTN. The clinical program includes two pivotal, registrational trials, the Phase 3 Launch-HTN trial and Phase 2 Advance-HTN trial, which support the durable and clinically meaningful reductions in systolic blood pressure by lorundrostat. Lorundrostat was well tolerated in both trials with a favorable safety profile.
