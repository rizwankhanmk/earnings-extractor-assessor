November 13, 2026

 

 

Dear Shareholder:

As we approach the holiday season and the end of another year, we are grateful for the trust
and support of our shareholders, customers, associates, and communities. That support has
sustained Uwharrie through both good times and challenging ones, and we do not take it for
granted. For more than four decades, we have remained focused on a simple purpose: helping
people prosper and helping the communities we serve grow stronger.

That responsibility requires us to be good stewards of the resources entrusted to us and to
manage the Company with a strategic perspective. We are pleased to report that as of
September 30, 2026, total assets reached $1.23 billion, an increase of $15 million compared to
the same period in 2025. Our loan portfolio grew by $27 million to $709 million, supported by
core deposit growth of $14 million year-over-year. Net income for the nine months ending
September 30, 2026, was $10 million, up nearly 21% from $8.3 million for the same nine
months in 2025.

In light of this continued progress, the Board of Directors has declared a 3% stock dividend
payable on November 23, 2026, to shareholders of record as of November 5, 2026. The
dividend will be issued electronically in book-entry form. This stock dividend reflects the
growth of the Company and provides our shareholders with something important, choice and
flexibility. Shareholders who do not need current income may retain their dividend shares and
continue building their ownership in Uwharrie, while those who desire current income may
choose to sell the shares and receive the value in cash. This approach allows each shareholder
to make the decision that best fits his or her individual circumstances and long-term financial
objectives.

We are encouraged by our progress, but we also recognize the challenges facing many of our customers and businesses. Higher borrowing costs, elevated household and operating expenses, and continued economic uncertainty are affecting financial decisions throughout the communities we serve. These conditions have reduced mortgage banking income and continue to place pressure on funding costs and net interest margin. We will continue to manage these challenges carefully, with the same focus on asset quality, liquidity, capital strength, and sustained profitability that has served Uwharrie well over the years.

Thank you for the confidence you continue to place in Uwharrie. We are grateful for your investment, your support, and the opportunity to continue building our communities together. From all of us at Uwharrie, we wish you and your family a blessed holiday season and a happy and prosperous New Year.

Sincerely,

UWHARRIE CAPITAL CORP

 

 

 

/s/ Roger L. Dick

President and Chief Executive Officer
