Mach Natural Resources LP Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results; Declares Quarterly Cash Distribution of $0.22 Per Common Unit; Provides 2027 Outlook

 

OKLAHOMA CITY, Oklahoma, November 5, 2026 — Mach Natural Resources LP (NYSE: MNR) (“Mach” or the “Company”) today reported financial and operating results for the three months ended September 30, 2026. The Company also announced its quarterly cash distribution and provided its full-year 2027 outlook.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

 

●Averaged total net production of 147 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day (“Mboe/d”), with Mach on track to achieve its full-year 2026 production guidance

 

●Lease operating expense of $6.49 per barrel of oil equivalent (“Boe”)

 

●Reported net income and Adjusted EBITDA(1) of $10 million and $183 million, respectively

 

●Generated net cash provided by operating activities of $135 million

 

●Incurred total development costs of $126 million

 

●Declared a quarterly cash distribution of $0.22 per common unit

 

Recent Highlights

 

●Post quarter-end, Mach reduced its operated rig count from three to one and remains positioned to achieve a full-year 2026 reinvestment rate under 50%

 

●Raised $35.5 million year-to-date 2026 in gross proceeds from the Company’s at-the-market equity program with net proceeds of $34.6 million applied toward debt reduction

 

“Mach is on track with its 2026 plan,” said Tom L. Ward, Chief Executive Officer. “With reduced capital spending currently underway in the fourth quarter, we remain positioned to achieve a full-year reinvestment rate under 50% which will translate to a significant increase in our fourth quarter per-unit cash distribution. Since 2024, we have delivered a yield nearly four times the peer average, and over the last five years we have generated a cash return on capital invested averaging 35%. Mach’s results are the reflection of a company built to deliver the highest possible cash distributions while maintaining a disciplined reinvestment rate, a lean cost structure and a strong balance sheet.”

 

Mr. Ward continued, “The durable asset base we have built since 2018 through nearly two dozen disciplined acquisitions will continue to reward our unitholders. With 2.8 million acres and years of high-quality inventory across three world-class basins, we have optimized our 2027 plan around the favorable oil outlook. We will allocate the vast majority of capital to our Oswego play and remain opportunistic on the material gas upside of our portfolio. We are confident in the flexibility of our business model and look forward to distinguished cash returns in 2027.”

 

 

 

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Mach reported total revenue and net income of $319 million and $10 million in the third quarter of 2026, respectively. Additionally, during the third quarter, the average realized price was $85.84 per barrel of oil, $2.17 per Mcf of natural gas, and $30.02 per barrel of natural gas liquids (“NGLs”). These prices exclude the effects of derivatives.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Operational Results

 

During the third quarter of 2026, Mach achieved average oil equivalent production of 147 Mboe/d, which consisted of 15% oil, 69% natural gas and 16% NGLs. Also, for the third quarter of 2026, Mach’s production revenues from oil, natural gas, and NGLs sales totaled $360 million, comprised of 49% oil, 34% natural gas, and 17% NGLs.

 

The Company spud 12 gross (8 net) operated wells and brought online 10 gross (6.3 net) operated wells in the third quarter of 2026.

 

Mach’s lease operating expense in the third quarter of 2026 was $88 million, or $6.49 per Boe. Mach incurred $57 million, or $4.24 per Boe, of gathering and processing expenses in the third quarter of 2026. Furthermore, during the third quarter of 2026, production taxes as a percentage of oil, natural gas, and NGL sales were approximately 4.5%, midstream operating profit was approximately $5 million, general and administrative expenses—excluding equity-based compensation of $3 million—was $6 million, and interest expense was $26 million.

 

In the third quarter of 2026, Mach’s total development costs were $126 million, including $120 million of upstream capital and $6 million of other capital (including midstream and land).

 

Distributions

Mach announced today that the board of directors of its general partner declared a quarterly cash distribution for the third quarter of 2026 of $0.22 per common unit. The quarterly cash distribution is to be paid on November 30, 2026, to common unitholders of record as of the close of trading on November 16, 2026.

 

2027 Outlook

 

Today the Company provided guidance for full-year 2027. The Company plans to spend $275 million to $325 million in total capital for development, while upholding Mach’s targeted reinvestment rate of no more than 50% of operating cash flow. The 2027 forecast for total production is expected to range between 134 MBoe/d to 142 MBoe/d. Additional details of Mach’s forward-looking guidance are available on the Company’s website at www.machnr.com.

Adjusted EBITDA(1)

 

Mach includes in this press release the supplemental non-GAAP financial performance measure Adjusted EBITDA and provides the Company’s calculation of Adjusted EBITDA and a reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA to net income, the Company’s most directly comparable financial measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as net (loss) income before (1) interest expense, net, (2) depreciation, depletion, amortization and accretion, (3) unrealized loss (gain) on derivative instruments, (4) impairment of oil and gas properties, (5) loss on debt extinguishment, (6) equity-based compensation expense, and (7) gain on sale of assets, net.

 

Adjusted EBITDA is used as a supplemental financial performance measure by Mach’s management and by external users of Mach’s financial statements, such as industry analysts, investors, lenders, rating agencies and others, to more effectively evaluate the Company’s operating performance and results of operation from period to period and against Mach’s peers without regard to financing methods, capital structure or historical cost basis. Mach excludes the items listed above from net income in arriving at Adjusted EBITDA because these amounts can vary substantially from company to company within the industry depending upon accounting methods and book values of assets, capital structures and the method by which the assets were acquired. Adjusted EBITDA is not a measurement of Mach’s financial performance under GAAP and should not be considered as an alternative to, or more meaningful than, net income as determined in accordance with GAAP or as indicators of the Company’s operating performance. Certain items excluded from Adjusted EBITDA are significant components in understanding and assessing a company’s financial performance, such as a company’s cost of capital and tax burden, as well as the historic costs of depreciable assets, none of which are reflected in Adjusted EBITDA. The Company’s presentation of Adjusted EBITDA should not be construed as an inference that Mach’s results will be unaffected by unusual items. The Company’s computations of Adjusted EBITDA may not be identical to other similarly titled measures of other companies.
