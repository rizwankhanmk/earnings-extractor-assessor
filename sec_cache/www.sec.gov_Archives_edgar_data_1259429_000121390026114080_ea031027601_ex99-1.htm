Oxford Square Capital Corp. Announces Net Asset Value and Selected Financial Results for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026 and Declaration of Distributions on Common Stock for the Months Ending January 31, February 28, and March 31, 2027

 

GREENWICH, CT – 10/30/2026 – Oxford Square Capital Corp. (NasdaqGS: OXSQ) (NasdaqGS: OXSQG) (NasdaqGS: OXSQH) (the “Company,” “we,” “us” or “our”) announced today its financial results and related information for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

·On October 28, 2026, our Board of Directors declared the following distributions on our common stock:

 

Month Ending Record Date Payment Date Amount Per Share
January 31, 2027 January 15, 2027 January 29, 2027 $0.015
February 28, 2027 February 12, 2027 February 26, 2027 $0.015
March 31, 2027 March 17, 2027 March 31, 2027 $0.015

 

·Net asset value (“NAV”) per share as of September 30, 2026, stood at $1.21, compared with a NAV per share on June 30, 2026 of $1.29.

 

·Net investment income (“NII”) was approximately $4.9 million, or $0.04 per share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with approximately $5.1 million, or $0.05 per share, for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

 

·Total investment income for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 amounted to approximately $9.9 million, compared with approximately $9.4 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

 

oFor the quarter ended September 30, 2026 we recorded investment income from our portfolio as follows:

 

§$5.6 million from our debt investments;

 

§$3.6 million from our CLO equity investments; and

 

§$0.6 million from other income.

 

·Our total expenses for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 were approximately $5.0 million, compared with total expenses of approximately $4.3 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

 

·As of September 30, 2026, the following metrics applied (note that none of these metrics represented a total return to shareholders):

 

oThe weighted average yield of our debt investments was 15.0% at current cost, compared with 14.9% as of June 30, 2026;

 

oThe weighted average effective yield of our CLO equity investments at current cost was 8.1%, which was approximately the same as of June 30, 2026;

 

oThe weighted average cash distribution yield of our cash income producing senior secured note investments at current cost was 8.1%, compared with 8.0% as of June 30, 2026; and

 

oThe weighted average cash distribution yield of our cash income producing CLO equity investments at current cost was 12.0%, compared with 12.4% as of June 30, 2026.

  

·For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, we recorded a net increase in net assets resulting from operations of approximately $1.6 million, consisting of:

 

oNII of approximately $4.9 million;

 

oNet realized losses of approximately $11.3 million; and

 

oNet unrealized appreciation of approximately $7.9 million. 

 

·During the third quarter of 2026, our investment activity consisted of purchases of approximately $20.8 million and repayments of approximately $10.3 million. No sales were made during the quarter.

 

·Our weighted average credit rating was 2.2 based on total fair value and 2.3 based on total principal amount as of September 30, 2026, compared with a weighted average credit rating of 2.1 based on total fair value and 2.2 based on total principal amount as of June 30, 2026.

 

·As of September 30, 2026, our preferred equity investments in one of our portfolio companies were on non-accrual status, which had an aggregate fair value of approximately $6.8 million.

 

·For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, we issued a total of approximately 14.1 million shares of common stock pursuant to an “at-the-market” offering. After deducting the sales agent’s commissions and offering expenses, this resulted in net proceeds of approximately $19.4 million. As of September 30, 2026, we had approximately 120.0 million shares of common stock outstanding.

About Oxford Square Capital Corp.

 

Oxford Square Capital Corp. is a publicly-traded business development company principally investing in syndicated bank loans and, to a lesser extent, debt and equity tranches of collateralized loan obligation (“CLO”) vehicles. CLO investments may also include warehouse facilities, which are financing structures intended to aggregate loans that may be used to form the basis of a CLO vehicle.
