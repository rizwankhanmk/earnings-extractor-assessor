TherapeuticsMD Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

BOCA RATON, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)—November 13, 2026 -- TherapeuticsMD, Inc. (“TherapeuticsMD” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: TXMD), a company that owns rights to pharmaceutical royalties, today reported financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Net Income from Continuing Operations

 

●Net income from continuing operations totaled $501 thousand, or $0.04 per basic and diluted common share, compared to income from continuing operations of $50 thousand, or $0.00 per basic and diluted common share for the third quarter of 2025.

 

License Revenues from Continuing Operations

 

●The Company recorded $1.3 million in license revenue for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $475 thousand, or 60.6%, compared to $784 thousand in license revenue for the third quarter of 2025. The increase is primarily attributable to the achievement of an IMVEXXY sales-based milestone by Knight relating to IMVEXXY sales for the year ended December 31, 2025, resulting in a $365 thousand milestone payment that was recognized and confirmed by Knight in the third quarter of 2026.
   
●Subsequent to September 30, 2026, Knight notified the Company that a net sales milestone related to IMVEXXY sales for the year ending December 31, 2026, had been achieved. Accordingly, the Company is entitled to a milestone payment and will recognize the related milestone revenue during the three months ending December 31, 2026.

 

Total Operating Expenses from Continuing Operations

 

●Total operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $1.7 million, an increase of $92 thousand, or 5.6%, compared to $1.6 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase is primarily due to higher legal and professional fees.

 

Evaluation of Strategic Alternatives

 

●The Company continues to evaluate a variety of strategic alternatives that may include, but are not limited to, an acquisition, merger, other business combination, sale of assets, or other strategic transactions involving the Company. Although the Company is exploring potential strategic alternatives, there can be no assurance of a transaction, a successful outcome of these efforts, or the form or timing of any such outcome. The Company has not established a timetable for completion of this exploration process and does not intend to disclose further developments unless and until it is determined that disclosure is appropriate or necessary.

 

Balance Sheet

 

●As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s cash and cash equivalents totaled $10.4 million.

 

 

 

 

About TherapeuticsMD

 

TherapeuticsMD was previously a women’s healthcare company with a mission of creating and commercializing innovative products to support the lifespan of women from pregnancy prevention through menopause. In December 2022, the Company changed its business to become a pharmaceutical royalty company, primarily collecting royalties from its licensees. The Company is no longer engaging in research and development or commercial operations.
