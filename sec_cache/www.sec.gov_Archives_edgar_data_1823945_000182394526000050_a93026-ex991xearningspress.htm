Blue Owl Capital Inc. Third Quarter 2026 Results

NEW YORK (October 29, 2026) – Blue Owl Capital Inc. (NYSE:OWL) (“Blue Owl”) today reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“Blue Owl demonstrated durability, resilience and an acceleration of growth during the third quarter. Our strategies continue to generate excellent investment results, and we believe the nearly $50 billion we’ve raised over the last twelve months reflects ongoing investor support and conviction around our disciplined approach to investing,” said Doug Ostrover and Marc Lipschultz, Co-CEOs of Blue Owl. “We had a record institutional capital raising quarter with growth coming from an increasingly broad set of strategies, investors, and geographies. During markets characterized by volatility and uncertainty, our focus on income-oriented returns with an emphasis on downside protection becomes even more top of mind for investors, and our scaled and partnership-driven approach to investing becomes increasingly valuable to our borrowers, tenants, and counterparties.”
Blue Owl issued its full detailed presentation of its third quarter ended September 30, 2026 results, which can be viewed through the Shareholders section of Blue Owl's website at https://ir.blueowl.com/Investors/events-and-presentations.
Dividend
Blue Owl declared a quarterly dividend of $0.23 per Class A Share, payable on November 23, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business on November 10, 2026.

About Blue Owl
Blue Owl (NYSE: OWL) is a leading asset manager that is redefining alternatives®. With $330 billion in assets under management as of September 30, 2026, we invest across three multi-strategy platforms: Credit, Real Assets and GP Strategic Capital. Anchored by a strong permanent capital base, we provide businesses with private capital solutions to drive long-term growth and offer institutional investors, individual investors, and insurance companies differentiated alternative investment opportunities that aim to deliver strong performance, risk-adjusted returns, and capital preservation.
