Century Casinos, Inc. Announces Restatement of Previously Issued Financial Statements and Preliminary Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results 

﻿

Colorado Springs, Colorado  – November 17, 2026 – Century Casinos, Inc. (the “Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) (Nasdaq Capital Market®: CNTY) today announced that it will restate certain previously issued financial statements. The restatement corrects an error in the Company's accounting for a minority interest in the entity that owns the real estate of the Nugget Casino Resort. The Company also provided preliminary financial results for its fiscal third quarter ended September 30, 2026.  

﻿

Preliminary Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results (Unaudited):

﻿

Preliminary third quarter 2026 (unaudited) estimates, presented on an as-reported basis, are:

﻿

·

Approximately $60.2 million in cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026 (compared to $68.9 million as of December 31, 2025), excluding $3.4 million in cash and cash equivalents held for sale in Canada;

·

Approximately $158.4 million in net operating revenue (compared to $153.7 million in the third quarter 2025); and

·

Approximately $20.7 million in earnings from operations (compared to $17.1 million in the third quarter 2025).

﻿

The Company prepared these preliminary figures on the same basis as its previously reported results, before any restatement adjustments. The restatement may change the figures and the presentation of noncontrolling interest. The prior-period comparisons come from financial statements that the Company has concluded should no longer be relied upon, as described below.

﻿

Restatement of Previously Issued Financial Statements:

﻿

What happened. On November 13, 2026, the Audit Committee of the Company's Board of Directors (the "Audit Committee") concluded that investors should no longer rely on the Company's previously issued financial statements described below. The Audit Committee, following discussion with the Company's management, independent registered public accounting firm, Ernst & Young LLP, and predecessor independent registered public accounting firm, Grant Thornton LLP, concluded that certain of the Company's previously issued financial statements should no longer be relied upon due to errors identified in the accounting for the 50% interest owned by Marnell Gaming, LLC (“Marnell”) in Smooth Bourbon, LLC (“Smooth Bourbon”), a consolidated variable interest entity which owns the real estate assets underlying the Nugget Casino Resort in Reno-Sparks, Nevada (the "Misstatement").

 

Background. The Company purchased its 50% membership interests in Smooth Bourbon on April 1, 2022. The Company acquired the Nugget on April 3, 2023, and began consolidating Smooth Bourbon as a variable interest entity on that date. The Company has a controlling financial interest in Smooth Bourbon because the Nugget is its primary beneficiary.

﻿

Buy-sell rights. The Company and Marnell entered into an operating agreement on April 1, 2022. Under that agreement:

﻿

·

The Company may purchase Marnell's noncontrolling membership interests in Smooth Bourbon.

·

After April 1, 2027, either the Company or Marnell may cause a sale of Smooth Bourbon.

·

If either party initiates a sale and the sale does not occur within the 18-month sale period, Marnell may require the Company to purchase Marnell's noncontrolling interests (the "Marnell Put").

·

The earliest exercise date of the Marnell Put is October 1, 2028.

﻿

Because of these features, the Audit Committee concluded that the Company should not have recorded it as a noncontrolling interest.

﻿

Periods affected. The Misstatement requires the Company to correct the following financial statements (collectively, the "Restated Periods"):

﻿

·

the audited consolidated financial statements as of and for the years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024, included in the Company's Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025 (the "Annual Report");

·

the unaudited condensed consolidated quarterly financial information for the quarters ended March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2026; and

·

the unaudited condensed consolidated quarterly financial information for each quarterly period in the years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024.

﻿

The quarterly information appears in the Company's Quarterly Reports on Form 10-Q for those periods (the "Quarterly Reports" and, together with the Annual Report, the "Affected Reports").

﻿

Investors should no longer rely on the previously issued financial statements included in the Affected Reports.

﻿

Next steps. The Audit Committee concluded that the Company should correct the Misstatement by restating the financial statements for the Restated Periods. The Company intends to file amendments to the Annual Report and to the Quarterly Reports for the periods ended March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2026 as soon as practicable. The amended Annual Report will include restated financial statements for the years ended December 31, 2024 and 2025 and for each quarterly period in those years.

﻿

Internal Control Over Financial Reporting:

﻿

Management is assessing how the restatement affects the Company's internal control over financial reporting and its disclosure controls and procedures. The Company expects to report at least one additional material weakness once it completes its analysis of the cause of the restatement.
