Warrior Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Generates $166.0 million of operating cash flows leading to recently announced $2.00 per-share special dividend

 

Sales and production volumes increase 48% and 36% year-over-year, respectively

 

Net income rises to $49.3 million and Adjusted EBITDA increases 76% to $124.0 million

 

BROOKWOOD, AL - November 4, 2026 - Warrior Met Coal, Inc. (NYSE: HCC) (“Warrior” or the “Company”) today announced results for the third quarter of 2026. Warrior is the leading dedicated U.S.-based producer and exporter of high-quality steelmaking coal for the global steel industry.

 

“We delivered strong sales volumes in the third quarter as Blue Creek continued to expand our production base and strengthen our cash-generating capacity,” commented Walt Scheller, CEO of Warrior. “Higher volumes and improved pricing generated significant free cash flow and further strengthened our balance sheet, in line with our priorities for the next phase of our growth strategy.

“Approximately one year after commencing longwall operations at Blue Creek, eight months ahead of schedule, and only two quarters after completing construction ahead of schedule, we are already seeing the benefits of this transformational investment in our cash generation and our ability to return excess cash to stockholders. The special dividend announced this quarter represents an important milestone for Warrior and demonstrates how quickly Blue Creek is beginning to translate into value for our stockholders," Mr. Scheller concluded.

 

Highlights

 

•
Generated cash flows from operations of $166.0 million and free cash flow of $138.9 million in the third quarter of 2026.

 

•
Increased sales volumes 48% to 3.5 million short tons and increased production volumes 36% to 3.1 million short tons driven by contributions from the Blue Creek mine.

 

 

 

•
Delivered strong net income of $49.3 million and Adjusted EBITDA increased to $124.0 million, up 76%, reflecting the continued ramp-up in the profitability contribution from the Blue Creek mine and improved steelmaking coal prices.

 

•
Declared a special dividend of $2.00 per share in addition to the regular quarterly dividend of $0.08 per share.

 

Financial and Operating Results

Total revenues were $507.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, reflecting a 48% increase in sales volumes and a 6% increase in the average net selling price. The increase in the average net selling price reflects improved steelmaking coal prices for both Premium Low-Vol (“PLV”) and High-Vol A coals, but was partially offset by higher Pacific freight costs and associated demurrage rate increases resulting from the ongoing conflict in the Middle East, as well as vessel loading delays at the port. Warrior’s average gross selling price realization declined to approximately 66% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 83% in the third quarter of 2025, driven primarily by a 25% higher sales mix of High-Vol A steelmaking coal predominantly sold into the Pacific Basin at higher freight rates and at lower second tier price relativities to the Platts PLV FOB Australian index.

 

Cost of sales for the third quarter of 2026 was $368.5 million. Cash cost of sales (free-on-board port) per short ton increased 4% to $104.98, primarily reflecting the impact of higher year-over-year steelmaking coal prices on Warrior’s variable cost structure (including transportation and royalties), lower production volumes at Mine No. 7, which resulted in production costs being absorbed over fewer tons, and inflationary cost increases. These increases were partially offset by the lower-cost contribution from the Blue Creek mine and the benefit of the Section 45X Advanced Manufacturing Production Tax Credit.

 

Cash Flow and Liquidity

Higher earnings and improved operating performance generated cash flows from operations of $166.0 million and free cash flow of $138.9 million in the third quarter of 2026. Net working capital, excluding cash, for the third quarter decreased $33.7 million from the second quarter of 2026, primarily due to lower inventories reflecting Warrior’s proactive decision to monetize surplus amounts of High-Vol A coal inventories.

 

The Company’s total liquidity as of September 30, 2026 was $577.6 million, consisting of cash and cash equivalents of $437.1 million, and available liquidity under its ABL Facility of $140.5 million (which is net of outstanding letters of credit of $2.5 million).

 

Capital Allocation

On October 27, 2026, the Board declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.08 per share, which the Company plans to distribute on November 13, 2026, to stockholders of record as of the close of business on November 6, 2026.

About Warrior

Warrior is a U.S.-based, environmentally and socially minded supplier to the global steel industry. It is dedicated entirely to mining non-thermal metallurgical (met) steelmaking coal used as a critical component of steel production by metal manufacturers in Europe, South America and Asia. Warrior is a large-scale, low-cost producer and exporter of premium quality met coal, also known as hard-coking coal (HCC), operating highly efficient longwall operations in its underground mines based in Alabama. The HCC that Warrior produces from the Blue Creek coal seam contains very low sulfur and has strong coking properties. The premium nature of Warrior’s HCC makes it ideally suited as a base feed coal for steel makers. For more information, please visit www.warriormetcoal.com.
