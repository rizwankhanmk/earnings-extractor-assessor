Immuneering Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Updates

- Phase 2 pancreatic cancer follow-up extended as patients on atebimetinib combinations live longer than projected -

- Updated Phase 2 atebimetinib + mGnP pancreatic cancer data (N=55) expected in the first half of 2027 -

- Phase 3 MAPKeeper 301 enrollment ahead of schedule; enrollment target expanding from 510 to 570 patients; potential to accelerate current mid-2028 topline data guidance -

NEW YORK, NY, October 29, 2026 - Immuneering Corporation (Nasdaq: IMRX), a late-stage clinical oncology company focused on keeping cancer patients alive and helping them thrive, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided business updates.

“We are thrilled to share that follow-up in our Phase 2 trial in first-line pancreatic cancer is being extended because patients are living longer than projected. We now plan to provide an update on topline data from the atebimetinib + mGnP arm of that trial in the first half of 2027. We are also excited to report that enrollment in our pivotal Phase 3 MAPKeeper 301 trial is ahead of schedule. This strong momentum enables us to expand the enrollment target and potentially accelerate our expected mid-2028 topline data readout. We have updated our cash runway guidance to the fourth quarter of 2028 to reflect our Phase 2 extension, accelerated and expanded Phase 3 enrollment, and commercial readiness activities,” said Ben Zeskind, Ph.D., CEO of Immuneering. “We are seeing growing recognition within the medical community of atebimetinib’s differentiated mechanism of action as demonstrated by the strong engagement with our presentations at recent medical meetings, including the 2026 AACR Conference on Pancreatic Cancer and ESMO Congress 2026. The 17.3 months median overall survival with excellent tolerability that we reported at ASCO is unprecedented among agents targeting this pathway, and we are excited to share updated clinical data in the first half of 2027.”

Corporate Highlights

•Phase 2 Pancreatic Cancer Trial Extended; Topline Data Update Expected in 1H 2027. The Company plans to share further data from the mGnP arm of its Phase 2 PDAC trial (N = 55) in 1H 2027. Follow-up has been extended as patients are living longer than projected.

•Phase 3 MAPKeeper 301 Enrollment Updated. Enrollment is ahead of schedule for Immuneering’s Phase 3 MAPKeeper 301 trial, enabling the Company to expand the trial to approximately 570 patients and potentially accelerate its current mid-2028 topline data readout guidance.


•Publication in Cancer Research Detailed Atebimetinib’s Unique Attributes as the First/Only Third Generation MEK Inhibitor, Including Broad, Durable Preclinical Activity and Favorable Tolerability Across RAS- and RAF-Mutant Tumors via Deep Cyclic Inhibition In October, atebimetinib was featured on the cover of Cancer Research in a paper entitled “Dual-MEK Inhibitor Atebimetinib Displays Broad Activity in RAS- and RAF-Mutant Tumors via Deep Cyclic Inhibition and Resisting RAF Bypass” and also described in a spotlight commentary entitled “MEKing Room: On the Quest for New Sources of Therapeutic Index in RAS/MAPK-Driven Cancers” both highlighting the Company’s publication in the same issue, which included data showing broad antitumor activity across KRAS-, NRAS-, and BRAF-mutant models, including colorectal, lung, and melanoma, by counteracting the RAF-mediated bypass signaling that has constrained other MEK inhibitors.

•Presented Data at the 2026 AACR Conference on Pancreatic Cancer Supporting Atebimetinib’s Mechanisms Designed to Extend Survival in Pancreatic Cancer. In September, the Company presented data showing that stable or increased weight was twice as common among pancreatic cancer patients in an atebimetinib + mGnP trial, compared to those in a benchmark study, and that patients with stable or increased weight at three months had significantly longer overall survival than patients who lost weight at three months.

•Presented MAPKeeper 301 Trial Design at ESMO Congress 2026. In October, Immuneering presented the design for its pivotal Phase 3 trial evaluating atebimetinib in combination with modified gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel versus standard-of-care chemotherapy in first-line metastatic pancreatic cancer at ESMO.

Immuneering is planning for several near-term anticipated milestones, including:

•4Q 2026 (SITC): Preclinical data for atebimetinib in combination with anti-PD-1 in lung cancer to be shared at the Society for Immunotherapy of Cancer (SITC) Annual Meeting.

•4Q 2026: Dose the first patient in Phase 2 trial of atebimetinib + anti-PD-1 (cemiplimab) in lung cancer, with a preliminary data readout expected in late-2027.

•1H 2027: Updated 1L PDAC Phase 2 atebimetinib + mGnP data (N=55).

•Mid-2027: Begin IND-enabling studies for our next DCI product candidate program.

•Late-2027: Preliminary data readout expected from Phase 2 trial of atebimetinib + anti-PD-1 (cemiplimab) in lung cancer.

•Mid-2028: Topline data readout from the MAPKeeper 301 trial.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

Cash Position: Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of September 30, 2026 were $166.1 million, compared with $217.0 million as of December 31, 2025.

Research and Development (R&D) Expenses: R&D expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $20.7 million, compared with $10.9 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in R&D expenses was primarily attributable to higher clinical costs related to the Company’s lead program and increased personnel to support ongoing research and development activities.


General and Administrative (G&A) Expenses: G&A expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $5.1 million, compared with $4.5 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in G&A expenses was primarily attributable to an increase in the Company’s stock-based compensation costs and employee-related costs in connection with general and administrative functions.

Net Loss: Net loss attributable to common stockholders was $24.2 million, or $0.37 per share, for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $15.0 million, or $0.38 per share, for the third quarter ended September 30, 2025. 

Financial Guidance

Based on cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of September 30, 2026, and current operating plans, the Company expects its cash runway to be sufficient to fund operations into the fourth quarter of 2028. The updated cash runway reflects the Phase 2 trial extension, accelerated and expanded Phase 3 enrollment, and commercial readiness activities.

About Immuneering Corporation

Immuneering is a late-stage clinical oncology company dedicated to keeping cancer patients alive and helping them thrive, with an initial focus on patients with RAS, RAF, and other MAPK-driven cancers. The Company is developing an entirely new category of cancer medicines, Deep Cyclic Inhibitors, designed to improve overall survival by three mechanisms: shrinking tumors durably with less resistance, preserving body mass by countering cachexia, and minimizing side effects to maximize performance status and combinability. Immuneering’s lead product candidate, atebimetinib, is an oral, once-daily Deep Cyclic Inhibitor of MEK, designed to improve survival across many cancer indications. The Company is conducting a global randomized pivotal trial, MAPKeeper 301, evaluating atebimetinib in combination with chemotherapy in first-line pancreatic cancer patients. The Company’s development pipeline also includes additional combination opportunities and preclinical stage programs. For more information, please visit www.immuneering.com.
