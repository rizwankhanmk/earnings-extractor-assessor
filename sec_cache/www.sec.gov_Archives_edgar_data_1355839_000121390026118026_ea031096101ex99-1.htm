Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Infrastructure Demand Drives Revenue Growth of More Than 75%;

Generated $5.6 million of Operating Cash Flow;

Company Positioned for Future Recurring Revenue Expansion

 

JERICHO, NY / ACCESS Newswire / November 10, 2026 / Intelligent Protection Management Corp. (“IPM,” “we,” “us,” “our” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: IPM), a managed technology solutions provider focused on enterprise cybersecurity and cloud infrastructure, today announced its financial results for the three months and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Management Discussion

 

Jason Katz, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, said: “Financial results for the third quarter of 2026 reflected exceptional customer demand and continued execution of our growth strategy, resulting in total revenue increasing more than 75% to approximately $11.0 million compared to the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, total revenue increased 36% year-over-year to $23.8 million. Our performance during the quarter was driven primarily by increased procurement revenue of approximately 271% compared to the prior year period. This core area represented a larger portion of our overall revenue mix compared to prior periods, as customers accelerated infrastructure investments in cybersecurity, cloud modernization, data center expansion and artificial intelligence. Additionally, we successfully fulfilled a significant customer order during the third quarter, further demonstrating our ability to execute on large-scale opportunities and meet customer demand.”

 

Mr. Katz continued: “Standard procurement gross margins trend lower than the rest of our business. At the same time, procurement services can provide an entry point for broader customer relationships. Our professional services help customers plan their technology investments; our procurement services source the infrastructure; and our managed IT security services, private cloud, and managed backup and disaster recovery services create opportunities to support those environments over time. Our objective is to convert these opportunities into recurring engagements, recognizing that conversion is not automatic and depends on customer needs and execution.”

 

Mr. Katz added: “While it can take time, we are making progress in expanding our recurring business. Core managed services revenue from non-affiliated customers increased 33% over the prior-year period. That result supports our focus on deepening customer relationships and diversifying our revenue base. We remain focused on expanding higher-margin recurring engagements across our service portfolio, while continuing to execute on professional services and procurement opportunities to deepen customer relationships and increase long-term customer value.”

 

 

 

 

Mr. Katz concluded: “Our balance sheet remains strong, providing the financial flexibility necessary to support organic growth initiatives while pursuing strategic opportunities that enhance our long-term growth profile. During the quarter, we incurred approximately $195,000 of acquisition-related expenses associated with the recently announced AdTech acquisition. Once completed, we believe the transaction can expand our customer reach and create opportunities to introduce IPM’s managed IT security services, private cloud, managed backup and disaster recovery, professional services, and procurement offerings across a larger combined customer base. We remain committed to disciplined capital allocation and creating long-term shareholder value. We are encouraged by the momentum we are seeing across the business and remain focused on executing our long-term strategy.”

Financial Results: Three and Nine Months ended September 30, 2026

 

For the three months ended September 30, 2026, total revenue increased by approximately 76% to $11.0 million from $6.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. This increase was primarily driven by increased procurement revenue related to customer investments in infrastructure, as well as increased professional services and managed information technology revenue, attributed to both new customers and the expansion of services sold to existing customers, partially offset by a decrease in subscription revenue. Total revenue by revenue component for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, was as follows:

 

●Managed information technology revenue, which consists of revenue from our managed IT security services, managed backup and disaster recovery solutions and web hosting, was $3.9 million, an increase of 1.8% from Q3 2025.

 

●Procurement revenue was $6.3 million, an increase of 271% from Q3 2025.

 

●Professional services revenue was $545,317, an increase of 14.5% from Q3 2025.

 

●Subscription revenue was $243,886, a decrease of 10.4% from Q3 2025.

 

Revenue from NewtekOne, Inc., a related party, was impacted by its ongoing initiatives to reduce information technology spending. We partially offset this decline in revenue through growth in recurring revenue from customers who are not related parties, including the addition of new customer relationships and service contracts during the period. Revenue from our core managed information technology services, which consists of revenue from our managed IT security services and managed backup and disaster recovery solutions, from non-affiliated customers increased by 33% compared to the prior year period.

 

●Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 totaled $0.8 million compared to net loss of $1.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025;

 

●Adjusted EBITDA for the three months ended September 30, 2026 totaled positive $0.2 million compared to negative $0.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025;

 

●Cash provided by operations totaled $5.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to cash provided by operations of $0.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily related to our sourcing of product in connection with the increased procurement revenue;

 

●Deferred revenue was $4.9 million as of September 30, 2026, which will be recognized as revenue in future quarters as products and/or services are installed; and

 

●At September 30, 2026, we had $12.5 million of cash and cash equivalents on our balance sheet and no long-term debt.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

The Company has provided in this release Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure, to supplement the consolidated financial statements, which are prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”). Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) adjusted to exclude interest (income) expense, net, other (income) expense, net, income tax (benefit) expense, depreciation and amortization expense, stock-based compensation expense, net loss from discontinued operations, impairment loss in connection with the Divestiture, litigation expenses relating to the Cisco ManyCam Litigation (as defined below) and acquisition-related expenses associated with the AdTech Acquisition (as defined below), as each are applicable to the periods presented. Prior to the fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026, we did not exclude acquisition-related expenses associated with the AdTech Acquisition in calculating Adjusted EBITDA. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the acquisition-related expenses of $277,844 include $82,819 incurred in the three months ended June 30, 2026, which amount was not adjusted in the prior quarter as it was not material. However, after reevaluation, we have determined that presenting Adjusted EBITDA without excluding such costs provides less valuable information about our core operations. As a result, beginning with the fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026, acquisition-related expenses associated with the AdTech Acquisition are excluded from the calculation of Adjusted EBITDA.

 

Management uses Adjusted EBITDA internally in analyzing the Company’s financial results to assess operational performance and to determine the Company’s future capital requirements. The presentation of this financial information is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP. The Company believes that both management and investors benefit from referring to Adjusted EBITDA in assessing its performance and when planning, forecasting and analyzing future periods. The Company believes Adjusted EBITDA is useful to investors and others to understand and evaluate the Company’s operating results and it allows for a more meaningful comparison between the Company’s performance and that of competitors. Our use of Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an analytical tool, and you should not consider this performance measure in isolation from or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP. Some of these limitations are that Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect, among other things: interest income, net; the benefit or provision for income taxes; other income, net; litigation expenses incurred in connection with our patent defense against Cisco Systems, Inc. and Cisco Technology, Inc. (the “Cisco ManyCam Litigation”); acquisition-related expenses associated with the Company’s pending acquisition of the IT and infrastructure solutions business of Advanced Technology Distributors Inc. (the “AdTech Acquisition"); cash capital expenditures for assets underlying depreciation and amortization expense that may need to be replaced or for new capital expenditures; and the potentially dilutive impact of stock-based compensation. Other companies, including companies in our industry, may calculate Adjusted EBITDA differently, which reduces its usefulness as a comparative measure.

 

Because of these limitations, you should consider Adjusted EBITDA along with other financial performance measures, including total revenues, subscription revenue, deferred revenue, net income (loss), cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash, net cash used in operating activities and our financial results presented in accordance with GAAP.
