Third Quarter 2026 Results
Commenting on the quarter, Thomas McDonnell, Chief Executive Officer of Barings BDC, stated, “Our third quarter results reflect the stability of our predominantly senior secured portfolio. Net investment income of $0.26 per share fully covered our dividend, and NAV per share was largely unchanged at $10.92. We remained active in the market, funding $196 million of investments, including 20 new portfolio companies, and grew the portfolio to $2.5 billion. We believe our liquidity position and the breadth of the Barings platform position us well to continue delivering value for our shareholders.”

During the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported total investment income of $64.3 million, net investment income of $27.2 million, or $0.26 per share, and a net increase in net assets resulting from operations of $24.6 million, or $0.24 per share.
Net asset value (“NAV”) per share decreased by $0.02 from the prior quarter to $10.92 as of September 30, 2026, primarily reflecting net realized losses of $0.05 per share, partially offset by net unrealized appreciation of $0.02 per share.

Liquidity and Capitalization
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had cash and foreign currencies of $76.2 million (including restricted cash of $25.0 million), $334.7 million of borrowings outstanding under its $821.5 million senior secured credit agreement, $1,132.5 million aggregate principal amount of unsecured notes outstanding and a net receivable from unsettled transactions of $1.4 million.
On October 30, 2026, the Company priced Barings BDC CLO 2026-1, an approximately $396.3 million collateralized loan obligation (“CLO”). The CLO consists of $304.0 million of rated secured notes sold to third-party investors and $92.3 million of subordinated notes retained by the Company. The floating rate notes carry a weighted average interest rate of Term SOFR plus 1.64%. The transaction is expected to close in mid-November 2026, subject to customary closing conditions. The Company intends to use the net proceeds, together with borrowings under its senior secured credit agreement, to repay in full its $350.0 million of 3.300% notes due November 23, 2026.

Dividend Reinvestment Plan
Barings BDC has adopted a dividend reinvestment plan (“DRIP”) that provides for reinvestment of dividends and distributions on behalf of its stockholders, unless a stockholder elects to receive cash. As a result, when the Company declares a cash dividend or distribution, stockholders who have not opted out of the DRIP will have their cash dividends or distributions automatically reinvested (net of applicable withholding tax) in additional shares of the Company’s common stock, rather than receiving cash.
When the Company declares and pays dividends and distributions, it determines the allocation of the distribution between current income, accumulated income, capital gains and return of capital on the basis of accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP”). At each year end, the Company is required for tax purposes to determine the allocation based on tax accounting principles. Due to differences between GAAP and tax accounting principles, the portion of each dividend distribution that is ordinary income, capital gain or return of capital may differ for GAAP and tax purposes. The tax status of the Company’s distributions can be found on the Investor Relations page of its website.
Subsequent Events
Subsequent to September 30, 2026, the Company made approximately $70.9 million of new commitments, of which $36.2 million closed and funded. The $36.2 million of investments consists of $35.8 million of first lien senior secured debt investments and $0.4 million of equity investments. The weighted average yield of the debt investments was 11.1%. In addition, the Company funded $35.0 million of previously committed revolvers and delayed draw term loans.

About Barings BDC
Barings BDC, Inc. (NYSE: BBDC) is a publicly traded, externally managed investment company that has elected to be treated as a business development company under the Investment Company Act of 1940. Barings BDC seeks to invest primarily in senior secured loans in middle-market companies that operate across a wide range of industries. Barings BDC’s investment activities are managed by its investment adviser, Barings, a leading global asset manager based in Charlotte, NC with $500 billion* of AUM firm-wide. For more information, visit www.baringsbdc.com.
About Barings
Barings is a $500 billion* global alternative asset manager that partners with institutional, insurance and wealth clients, and supports leading businesses with flexible financing solutions. The firm, a subsidiary of MassMutual with a minority investment from MS&AD, seeks to deliver excess returns by leveraging its global scale and capabilities across credit, real assets, capital solutions and emerging markets. Learn more at www.barings.com.
