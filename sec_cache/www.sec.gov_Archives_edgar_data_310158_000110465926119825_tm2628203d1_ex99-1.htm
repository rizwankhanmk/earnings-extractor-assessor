RAHWAY, N.J., Oct. 29, 2026 – Merck & Co., Inc., Rahway, N.J., USA (NYSE: MRK), known as MSD outside the United States and Canada, today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026.

 

“Our science-led strategy is transforming our business,” said Robert M. Davis, chairman and chief executive officer. “The potentially practice-changing advances in oncology presented at last weekend’s ESMO meeting, including new data for our antibody-drug conjugate sac-TMT and the first Phase 3 validation of a therapeutic cancer vaccine, further clinically de-risk the substantial long-term opportunity of our late-phase pipeline. With positive momentum across multiple therapeutic areas, I’m increasingly confident in our ability to deliver long-term value for patients and shareholders.”

GAAP Expense, EPS and Related Information

 

Gross margin was 72.9% for the third quarter of 2026 compared with 77.7% for the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to higher amortization of intangible assets, restructuring costs and production costs.

 

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses were $2.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 10% compared with the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher administrative costs, as well as higher promotional costs in support of product launches.

 

Research and development (R&D) expenses were $4.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 7% compared with the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to a $400 million charge related to a global license agreement with SciBrunch and higher clinical development spending, partially offset by lower restructuring costs and a $200 million reduction in R&D expenses as part of a funding agreement with Blackstone Life Sciences (Blackstone). R&D expenses in the third quarter of 2025 include a $300 million charge for a milestone payment to LaNova for the completion of the technology transfer for MK-2010.

 

Other (income) expense, net, was $27 million of expense in the third quarter of 2026 compared with $238 million of income in the third quarter of 2025. The unfavorability was primarily due to higher net interest expense.

Pipeline and Portfolio Highlights

 

In the third quarter, the Company achieved key regulatory and clinical milestones to meaningfully advance its broad and diverse pipeline.

 

·Oncology:

oU.S. Food and Drug Administration (U.S. FDA) approved WELIREG plus Lenvima, in collaboration with Eisai, for certain previously treated adult patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma with a clear cell component (ccRCC), based on Phase 3 LITESPARK-011 trial.

§Approval represents first approved combination of an oral hypoxia-inducible factor-2 alpha inhibitor and a multi-targeted vascular endothelial growth factor tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) for these patients.

oIn October, U.S. FDA accepted a Biologics License Application (BLA) seeking approval of sac-TMT for the treatment of adult patients with endometrial cancer who have previously received platinum containing chemotherapy and a programmed death receptor 1 or programmed death ligand 1 inhibitor, separately or in combination, based on Phase 3 TroFuse-005 trial.

§U.S. FDA set Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA) date of June 15, 2027. The Company is in discussions with the U.S. FDA and is hopeful for an earlier approval.

oIn October, European Commission (EC) approved KEYTRUDA plus Padcev as perioperative treatment for adults with resectable muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC), based on Phase 3 KEYNOTE-B15 trial.

oMinistry of Health, Labor and Welfare in Japan approved subcutaneous pembrolizumab and berahyaluronidase alfa injection under the trademark KEYJECT (marketed as KEYTRUDA QLEX in the U.S.) for all KEYTRUDA indications approved in Japan, based on Phase 3 MK-3475A-D77 trial.

oAt the European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO) Congress 2026 in October, positive new data were presented during three late-breaking Presidential Symposia across three Phase 3 trials from the Company’s robust oncology pipeline and portfolio, including:

§Results from Phase 3 TroFuse-005 trial evaluating sac-TMT for patients with advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer previously treated with chemotherapy and anti-PD-1/L1 immunotherapy.

§Results from Phase 3 INTerpath-001 trial, in collaboration with Moderna, Inc. (Moderna), evaluating adjuvant treatment with intismeran autogene (intismeran; V940 or mRNA-4157) in combination with KEYTRUDA.

§Results from Phase 3 KEYNOTE-C93 trial evaluating KEYTRUDA monotherapy versus platinum doublet chemotherapy as a first-line treatment for certain patients with mismatch repair deficient (dMMR) advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer who had not previously received systemic chemotherapy or who experienced recurrence more than six months after completing prior adjuvant therapy.

§In addition, data were presented from Phase 3 OptiTROP studies in China, led by Kelun-Biotech, evaluating sac-TMT in non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), including:

·Results from the OptiTROP-Lung06 study as first-line treatment for PD-L1-negative advanced non-squamous NSCLC.

·Final overall survival data from the OptiTROP-Lung04 study in epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR)-mutated NSCLC following progression on EGFR TKIs.

oIfinatamab deruxtecan (I-DXd) Biologics License Application (BLA), in collaboration with Daiichi Sankyo, for certain patients with previously treated extensive-stage small cell lung cancer (ES-SCLC) was voluntarily withdrawn.
   

·Vaccines and Infectious Diseases:

oU.S. FDA accepted a supplemental BLA (sBLA) for ENFLONSIA to expand its indication to include prevention of respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) lower respiratory tract disease (LRTD) in children under 2 years of age at increased risk for severe RSV disease through their second RSV season, based on Phase 3 SMART trial.

§U.S. FDA set PDUFA date of March 22, 2027.

§Applications to expand marketing authorization for ENFLONSIA for same population in Europe and Japan, based on Phase 3 SMART trial, have also been accepted.

U.S. FDA also accepted an sNDA to include 96-week data for IDVYNSO in certain virologically suppressed adults, based on the Phase 3 P051 and P052 trials.

oU.S. FDA approved an update to U.S. product label for WINREVAIR to include efficacy and safety data from the Phase 3 HYPERION trial evaluating adults recently diagnosed with pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH, World Health Organization [WHO] Group 1 pulmonary hypertension).

·Business Development:

oAnnounced an exclusive global license agreement with SciBrunch for MK-2690 (SPR2015), an investigational preclinical oral KRAS G12D (ON) inhibitor.

About Our Company

 

At Merck & Co., Inc., Rahway, N.J., USA, known as MSD outside of the United States and Canada, we are unified around our purpose: We use the power of leading-edge science to save and improve lives around the world.
