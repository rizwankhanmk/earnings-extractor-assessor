VITESSE ENERGY ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

GREENWOOD VILLAGE, Colo. – November 2, 2026 – Vitesse Energy, Inc. (NYSE: VTS) (“we,” “our,” “Vitesse,” or the “Company”) today reported the Company’s third quarter 2026 financial and operating results and revised 2026 annual guidance.

THIRD QUARTER 2026 HIGHLIGHTS

•GAAP net loss of $36.4 million, including a non-cash unrealized loss on commodity derivatives of $46.6 million, and Adjusted Net Income(1) of $2.5 million

•Adjusted EBITDA(1) of $39.3 million

•Cash flow from operations of $39.4 million and Free Cash Flow(1) of $9.4 million

•Production of 16,824 barrels of oil equivalent (“Boe”) per day (60% oil)

•Total cash capital expenditures of $53.0 million, net of divestitures, including $25.9 million for the previously announced Denver-Julesburg (“DJ”) Basin Acquisition

•Total debt of $190.5 million and Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA ratio(1) of 1.3

(1) Non-GAAP financial measure; see reconciliation schedules at the end of this release

MANAGEMENT COMMENTS

“The third quarter was highlighted by the successful closing of the DJ Basin acquisition, which reinforces our ability to identify and capitalize on attractive investment opportunities,” stated Jamie Benard, Vitesse’s President and Chief Executive Officer. “We expect the transaction to add meaningful cash flow at a valuation accretive to all key metrics while expanding our position in the DJ Basin. This investment, along with anticipated continuation of accelerated drilling and completion activities, reflects Vitesse’s strategy of disciplined capital allocation, strengthening our portfolio, supporting our dividend, and generating sustainable returns for our stockholders.”

“Vitesse’s balance sheet remains conservative, with target leverage at or below 1.0x,” said James Henderson, Vitesse’s Chief Financial Officer. “Our hedge portfolio is designed to provide additional cash flow visibility and supports our confidence in maintaining our quarterly dividend and low leverage.”

SUBSEQUENT EVENT

•In October 2026, expanded availability under revolving credit facility by $15 million, with elected commitments and borrowing base equal to $290 million.



STOCKHOLDER RETURNS

On October 28, 2026, Vitesse declared its fourth quarter cash dividend of $0.4375 per share for stockholders of record as of December 15, 2026, which will be paid on December 31, 2026.

On September 30, 2026, the Company paid its third quarter cash dividend of $0.4375 per share to common stockholders of record as of September 15, 2026.

FINANCIAL AND OPERATING RESULTS

Third quarter net loss was $36.4 million and Adjusted Net Income was $2.5 million. Adjusted EBITDA was $39.3 million. See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below.

Oil and natural gas production for the third quarter of 2026 averaged 16,824 Boe per day, with the quarter only including one month of contribution from our Denver-Julesburg Basin Acquisition, which closed in September 2026. Oil represented 60% of production and 92% of total oil and natural gas revenue. Total revenue, including the effects of our realized hedges, was $71.0 million.

Vitesse’s average realized oil and natural gas prices before hedging were $82.56 per Bbl and $1.73 per Mcf, respectively, during the third quarter of 2026. The Company had hedges covering 76% of oil production and its realized oil price with hedging was $68.33 per Bbl. Its realized natural gas price with hedging was $2.06 per Mcf.

Lease operating expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were $19.2 million, or $12.42 per Boe. General and administrative expenses totaled $4.9 million, or $3.19 per Boe.

LIQUIDITY AND CAPITAL EXPENDITURES

As of September 30, 2026, Vitesse had $0.7 million in cash and $190.5 million of borrowings outstanding on its revolving credit facility. Vitesse had total liquidity of $85.2 million as of September 30, 2026, consisting of cash and $84.5 million of committed borrowing availability under its revolving credit facility.

In October 2026, the Company amended its revolving credit facility to increase elected commitments and borrowing base from $275 million to $290 million.

During the third quarter of 2026, Vitesse invested $26.9 million in development capital expenditures and acquired $26.2 million of oil and gas properties, including $25.9 million for the previously announced Denver-Julesburg Basin Acquisition. The closing payment was net of preliminary and customary purchase price adjustments and remains subject to post-closing cash settlements with the seller.

OPERATIONS UPDATE



As of September 30, 2026, the Company owned an interest in 314 gross (6.5 net) wells that were either drilling or in the completion phase, and another 349 gross (13.0 net) locations that had been permitted for development.

During the third quarter of 2026, Vitesse’s operators began drilling on 2.0 net wells and brought 2.0 net wells into production, compared to 1.8 net wells and 1.7 net wells, respectively, in the second quarter.

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES

Vitesse defines Adjusted Net Income (Loss) as net income (loss) before (i) non-cash gains and losses on unsettled derivative instruments, (ii) non-cash equity-based compensation, (iii) provision for (benefit from) income taxes, and (iv) certain other items such as material general and administrative costs, reduced by the estimated impact of income tax expense.



Net Debt is calculated by deducting cash on hand from the amount outstanding on our revolving credit facility as of the balance sheet or measurement date.

Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) before expenses for interest, income taxes, depletion, depreciation, amortization and accretion, and excludes non-cash equity-based compensation and non-cash gains and losses on unsettled derivative instruments in addition to certain other items such as material transaction and general and administrative costs.

Vitesse defines Free Cash Flow as cash flow from operations, adjusting for changes in operating assets and liabilities in addition to certain other items such as material general and administrative costs, less development of oil and gas properties.

Management believes the use of these non-GAAP financial measures provides useful information to investors to gain an overall understanding of financial performance. Specifically, management believes the non-GAAP financial measures included herein provide useful information to both management and investors by excluding certain items that management believes are not indicative of Vitesse’s core operating results. In addition, these non-GAAP financial measures are used by management for budgeting and forecasting as well as subsequently measuring Vitesse’s performance, and management believes it is providing investors with financial measures that most closely align to its internal measurement processes. A reconciliation of each of the non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measure is included below.
