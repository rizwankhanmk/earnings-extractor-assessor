REalloys Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Executes term sheet with the U.S. Army for a heavy rare earth processing facility at Tooele Army Depot; advances SRC metallization and Rare Earth Processing Facility programs; outlines steps toward targeted initial U.S. magnet production by 2030; $116.2 million of cash at quarter-end

 

●Subsequent to quarter-end, executed a non-binding term sheet with the Department of the Army setting out the principal terms for negotiating a definitive Enhanced Use Lease for a heavy rare earth processing facility on approximately 200 acres at Tooele Army Depot, Utah

 

●Advanced the Heavy Rare Earth Metallization Facility, targeting commissioning in the first quarter of 2028 with targeted capacity of approximately 50 tonnes of annual dysprosium and terbium oxide

 

●Targeting North American rare earth metal production, with access to NdPr metallization capability at SRC expected beginning in the first quarter of 2027 and dysprosium and terbium metal output from the Company’s metallization facility expected in the first half of 2028, addressing the limited North American production of rare earth metals

 

●Provided additional detail on the Company’s mine-to-magnet strategy and the steps toward a targeted initial U.S.-based production of approximately 3,000 tonnes per year of sintered NdFeB magnets no later than 2030

 

●Plans to dedicate approximately $10 million through 2027 to research and development, piloting, and product qualification with the Euclid facility at the center of these activities

 

●Ended the quarter with $116.2 million of cash and a virtually debt-free balance sheet, with spending sequenced to project milestones

 

EUCLID, Ohio, November 13, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — REalloys Inc. (Nasdaq: ALOY) (the “Company” or “REalloys”), today reported results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Net revenues were $0.4 million, compared with $0.2 million in the third quarter of 2025, and the Company reported a net loss of $30.5 million, or $0.44 per diluted share, compared with a net loss of $25.6 million, or $0.58 per diluted share, in the prior-year quarter.

 

The net loss was driven primarily by $25.3 million of non-cash stock-based compensation associated with director, officer and consultant equity awards, primarily granted in connection with the Company’s February 2026 transition to a Nasdaq-listed public company.

 

“This quarter we moved further from planning into execution. The term sheet with the U.S. Army at Tooele, the initial funding of expanded NdPr metallization capacity at SRC and continued progress on our heavy rare earth metallization facility advance our path toward North American rare earth metal production, which we are targeting to begin in 2027. With Department of War sourcing restrictions taking effect on January 1, 2027, we believe demand for secure, non-Chinese supplies of NdPr, dysprosium and terbium will continue to grow. We are managing our capital with discipline, sequencing spending to technical and commercial milestones, and we expect to fund our committed programs from existing cash as we take the next steps toward our targeted initial U.S. magnet production by 2030.”

 

— Leonard Sternheim, Chief Executive Officer of REalloys

 

 

 

 

Third Quarter Financial Highlights

 

●The Company’s cash balance as of September 30, 2026 was approximately $116.2 million

 

●Deployed $16.5 million to SRC programs during the first nine months of 2026, including $11.6 million toward the Heavy Rare Earth Metallization Facility, $3.7 million toward the Rare Earth Processing Facility upgrade and $1.2 million toward process development, in line with the Company’s capital plans

 

●Maintained a strong, virtually debt-free balance sheet, against $197.4 million of assets and $123.4 million of working capital

 

Euclid facility sales drove revenue growth with approximately 36% gross margin. General and administrative expense for the quarter included $25.3 million of non-cash stock-based compensation; excluding it, general and administrative expense was approximately $4.9 million (a non-GAAP measure; see “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below).

 

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, net revenues were $1.9 million, compared with $0.6 million in the prior-year period, and net loss was $174.1 million, or $2.83 per diluted share, compared with a net loss of $29.5 million, or $0.75 per diluted share, in the prior-year period. The nine-month net loss included $158.2 million of non-cash charges, principally $139.2 million of stock-based compensation, $9.2 million of preferred share accretion, a $6.4 million impairment related to assets acquired through the Blackboxstocks merger and a $3.4 million change in the fair value of settled derivatives.

 

Strategic Projects Update and Outlook

 

Heavy Rare Earth Metallization Facility. The Company is advancing engineering and equipment procurement for its planned Heavy Rare Earth Metallization Facility, which is targeted for commissioning in the first quarter of 2028 and initial operations in the first half of 2028, with an estimated annual processing capacity of approximately 50 tonnes of combined dysprosium and terbium oxide feedstock.

 

SRC Rare Earth Processing Facility Upgrade. REalloys has initiated its planned funding under the upgrade agreement with SRC. Initial funding has been directed to completing NdPr metallization units, representing approximately one-third of the estimated total upgrade budget, which are expected to give the Company access to North American NdPr metal production capability beginning in the first quarter of 2027. Release of the remaining funds, which relate primarily to the separation system upgrade, has been deferred to 2027 while near-term activity at SRC focuses on commissioning and operating the existing facility. The Company does not expect the deferral to affect its supply of critical materials from SRC. The upgrade is intended to increase annual production capacity up to approximately 525 tonnes of NdPr metal, 30 tonnes of dysprosium oxide and 15 tonnes of terbium oxide, and REalloys has secured supply rights to approximately 80% of the facility’s output and has a Right of First Refusal on the uncommitted remaining output.

 

Together with SRC, the Company is advancing processing and separation trials using recycled mixed rare earth oxide and mixed rare earth carbonate feedstock through the first half of 2027. These trials were previously expected to be completed in 2026; the timing has shifted primarily due to feedstock alignment and procurement lead times, and the Company does not expect the change to affect its broader strategic execution plan. The Company expects initial separated oxide production from the SRC facility in the second half of 2027 and commercial intake of NdPr metal and dysprosium and terbium oxides from SRC to commence in the third quarter of 2027.

 

Integrated U.S. Separation, Metallization and Magnet Platform. REalloys has provided additional detail on the next stage of its mine-to-magnet strategy: a stage-gated, U.S.-based platform covering rare earth separation, metallization and sintered NdFeB magnet manufacturing, primarily serving U.S. defense, automotive and advanced industrial customers. The platform targets initial production of approximately 3,000 tonnes per year of magnets no later than 2030, supported by targeted annual production of up to 1,250 tonnes of NdPr metal, 60 tonnes of dysprosium metal and 25 tonnes of terbium metal. These capacity figures are design targets, not commitments, forecasts or guidance. The Company is evaluating Tooele Army Depot as a potential site for these facilities; because the term sheet contemplates a facility initially focused on dysprosium and terbium and a definitive lease may not accommodate all of the planned facilities, the Company is also evaluating alternative sites. The location, timing and capital requirements of these facilities have not yet been determined, and their development is expected to require significant additional financing. The Company plans to spend approximately $10 million through 2027 on research and development, piloting and product qualification, centered at the Euclid facility. The facility, product qualification, and cost estimation for separation, metallization, and magnet facilities, centered at the Euclid facility.is being assessed as a potential location for a future pilot-scale magnet manufacturing facility.

Capital Resources and Liquidity. As of September 30, 2026, remaining commitments under the SRC development agreements were approximately $56.0 million and are expected to be incurred through 2028 to bring the Company’s processing and metallization projects to commissioning. The Company expects to fund these requirements, its planned research and development, piloting and product qualification spending and general corporate costs from existing cash, without reliance on any specific future financing transaction. The Company is taking a disciplined, stage-gated approach to capital allocation: spending is sequenced to technical and commercial milestones, and commitments to larger-scale production facilities will follow the results of the Company’s research and development, piloting and product qualification work.

 

Recent Developments

 

U.S. Army Term Sheet at Tooele Army Depot. On November 3, 2026, REalloys announced that it and the Department of the Army had executed a non-binding term sheet setting out the principal terms for negotiating a definitive Enhanced Use Lease for a facility to process and refine critical minerals, including dysprosium and terbium, on approximately 200 acres at Tooele Army Depot in Utah. The indicative terms include an initial 50-year term with one 25-year renewal subject to Army consent, fair market value base rent plus participation rent (the rate and basis of which have not been agreed), and a right of first offer to the Army on facility output, without any obligation to purchase. The term sheet is non-binding, other than certain customary provisions, and execution of a definitive lease is subject to conditions including agreement on rent, Army acceptance of the Company’s development plan, completion of environmental reviews, adequate permit capacity, project financing, approval by the Company’s Board of Directors and required Army determinations and Congressional notifications.

 

About REalloys Inc.

 

REalloys Inc. (NASDAQ: ALOY) is a U.S.-based rare earth materials company executing a mine-to-magnet strategy across upstream feedstock, midstream separation and metallization, and downstream magnet manufacturing. REalloys is focused on delivering qualified, allied-nation rare earth metals and alloys, including dysprosium, terbium, and neodymium, to the U.S. Department of War, the U.S. Department of Energy, NASA, the U.S. Defense Industrial Base, and the broader U.S. Organic Industrial Base.
