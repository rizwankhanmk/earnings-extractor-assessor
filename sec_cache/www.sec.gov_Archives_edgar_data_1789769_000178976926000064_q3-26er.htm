Instil Bio Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and
Provides Corporate Update
DALLAS, TX, November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Instil Bio, Inc. (“Instil”) (Nasdaq: TIL), a biotechnology company building a portfolio of asset-focused subsidiaries to develop novel therapies for unmet medical needs, today reported its third quarter 2026 financial results and provided a corporate update.

Recent Highlights:
•Asset-Focused Development Model: By combining focused and experienced teams with strategic support from Instil, Instil intends to establish a portfolio of asset-focused development subsidiaries. To accomplish this strategy, Instil intends to internally develop or in-license programs, building a diversified portfolio of development candidates.
•Cash Position: Cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, marketable securities and long-term investments totaled approximately $67.1 million as of September 30, 2026, which Instil expects will fund its current operating plan through 2027.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results:
As of September 30, 2026, Instil had cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, marketable securities and long-term investments of $67.1 million, which consisted of $2.5 million in cash and cash equivalents, $0.4 million in restricted cash, $59.5 million in marketable securities and $4.6 million in long-term investments, compared to $76.3 million in cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash and marketable securities as of December 31, 2025, consisting of $6.6 million in cash and cash equivalents, $0.2 million in restricted cash and $69.5 million in marketable securities. Instil expects that its cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, marketable securities and long-term investments as of September 30, 2026 will enable it to fund its operating plan through 2027.
In-process research and development expenses were nil for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to nil and $10.0 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, respectively.
Research and development expenses were $0.2 million and $1.2 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively, compared to $9.1 million and $21.2 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, respectively.
General and administrative expenses were $5.8 million and $17.5 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively, compared to $6.3 million and $22.7 million, as corrected, for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, respectively.
Restructuring and impairment charges, net were a reversal of costs of $0.1 million and a charge of $1.1 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively, compared to nil and $16.6 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, respectively.

Net loss was $4.2 million and $13.0 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively, compared to $13.6 million and $63.5 million, as corrected, for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, respectively. Net loss per share, basic and diluted, was $0.62 and $1.92 for

the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively, compared to $2.01 and $9.58, as corrected, for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, respectively. Non-GAAP net loss per share, basic and diluted, was $0.48 and $1.32 for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively, compared to $1.75 and $6.00, as corrected, for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, respectively; see the reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP measures below.
In preparing its third-quarter 2026 financial statements, Instil identified errors in previously issued financial statements relating to property taxes on its Tarzana, California facility and the presentation of amounts reimbursed by the facility’s tenant, as well as certain other immaterial errors. The errors were not material to any previously issued financial statements, and no prior filing is being amended. Instil has corrected the prior-period information in its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and the 2025 amounts in this release are presented as corrected.
